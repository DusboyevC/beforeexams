# 5 Work, energy and power

## Key definitions
- **Work done**: force × displacement **in the direction of the force**: W = Fs cos θ.
- **Principle of conservation of energy**: energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one form to another; the total energy of a closed system is constant.
- **Kinetic energy**: Eₖ = ½mv².
- **Gravitational potential energy (change)**: ΔEₚ = mgΔh (uniform field near the Earth's surface).
- **Power**: rate of doing work or of transferring energy: P = W/t.
- **Efficiency**: useful energy (or power) output ÷ total energy (or power) input (× 100%).

## Important results
- **Work done by a gas**: W = pΔV (at constant pressure).
- **Power and velocity**: P = Fv, where F is the force in the direction of motion. (Derivation: P = W/t = Fs/t = Fv.)
- **Derivation of Eₖ = ½mv²**: a constant force F accelerates mass m from rest through distance s. W = Fs = mas, and v² = 2as, so W = ½mv².
- **Derivation of ΔEₚ = mgΔh**: lifting weight mg through height Δh at constant speed needs force mg, so W = mgΔh.

## Energy transfer problems
- Typical pattern: loss in Eₚ = gain in Eₖ + work done against friction or drag.
- Example: a 60 kg cyclist freewheels down a hill 12 m high, starting at 2 m s⁻¹, and reaches 14 m s⁻¹ at the bottom. Find the work done against resistance.
  - Loss of Eₚ = 60 × 9.81 × 12 = 7063 J
  - Gain of Eₖ = ½ × 60 × (14² − 2²) = 5760 J
  - Work against resistance = 7063 − 5760 = **1300 J** (2 s.f.)

## Power examples
- A car travelling at constant velocity: driving force = total resistive force, so the power needed is P = Fv.
- An electric motor lifting a load: useful power = mgh/t; efficiency = useful power ÷ electrical power input.

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- In W = Fs, s must be the displacement **in the direction of the force**; use cos θ if they are at an angle.
- Work done against friction ends up as **thermal energy** in the surroundings.
- Efficiency has no unit and must be less than 1 (or less than 100%).
- Convert km h⁻¹ to m s⁻¹ (÷ 3.6) before using P = Fv or Eₖ.

## Quick self-check
1. Derive P = Fv.
2. A crane lifts 500 kg through 20 m in 25 s. Find the useful output power. *(3.9 kW)*
3. A motor has an input power of 2.0 kW and an efficiency of 70%. Find the useful output power. *(1.4 kW)*
