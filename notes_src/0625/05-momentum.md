# 5 Momentum

This whole topic is Extended content.

## Momentum
- **Momentum** = mass × velocity: **p = mv**. Unit: kg m/s.
- Momentum is a vector, so direction matters. Choose one direction as positive.

## Impulse
- **Impulse** = force × time for which the force acts.
- **Impulse = FΔt = Δ(mv)**. Unit: N s.
- Impulse equals the change in momentum.

## Force and momentum
- Resultant force is the change in momentum per unit time: **F = Δp / Δt**.
- If the time of a collision is longer, the force is smaller for the same change in momentum.
- This explains **crumple zones, airbags and seat belts**. They increase the stopping time, so the force on the passenger is smaller.

## Conservation of momentum
- In a collision or explosion, the **total momentum before = total momentum after**, as long as no external force acts.

**Worked example 1: objects stick together**
A 1000 kg car at 8.0 m/s hits a stationary 600 kg car and they move together.
- Before: 1000 × 8.0 = 8000 kg m/s
- After: (1000 + 600) × v = 8000, so v = **5.0 m/s**

**Worked example 2: rebound**
A 0.25 kg ball hits a wall at 16 m/s and rebounds at 12 m/s.
- Take the original direction as positive. Change in momentum = 0.25 × (−12) − 0.25 × 16 = −3.0 − 4.0 = **−7.0 kg m/s**.
- The impulse from the wall has size **7.0 N s**.

**Worked example 3: explosion**
A 60 kg skater pushes a 40 kg skater. Both start at rest. The 40 kg skater moves off at 3.0 m/s.
- Total momentum stays zero: 60v + 40 × 3.0 = 0, so v = **−2.0 m/s** (2.0 m/s in the opposite direction).

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- A rebound reverses the sign of the velocity. This is the most common mistake in this topic.
- Add the masses when objects stick together.
- Give the direction when the question asks for velocity or momentum.

## Quick self-check
1. Find the momentum of a 70 kg runner at 8.0 m/s. *(560 kg m/s)*
2. A force of 50 N acts for 0.40 s. Find the impulse. *(20 N s)*
3. Why does an airbag reduce injury? *(It increases the stopping time, so the rate of change of momentum, and so the force, is smaller.)*
