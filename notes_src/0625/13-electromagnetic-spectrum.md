# 13 Electromagnetic spectrum

## The regions in order
**Increasing frequency, decreasing wavelength:**
radio waves → microwaves → infrared → visible light → ultraviolet → X-rays → gamma rays

- All electromagnetic waves travel at the same high speed in a vacuum.
- **Extended**: this speed is **3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**, and it is approximately the same in air.

## Uses
| Region | Uses |
|---|---|
| Radio waves | radio and television transmissions, astronomy, RFID |
| Microwaves | satellite television, mobile phones, microwave ovens |
| Infrared | electric grills, remote controllers, intruder alarms, thermal imaging, optical fibres |
| Visible light | vision, photography, illumination |
| Ultraviolet | security marking, detecting fake bank notes, sterilising water |
| X-rays | medical scanning, security scanners |
| Gamma rays | sterilising food and medical equipment, detection of cancer and its treatment |

## Harmful effects of too much exposure
- **Microwaves**: internal heating of body cells.
- **Infrared**: skin burns.
- **Ultraviolet**: damage to surface cells and eyes, leading to skin cancer and eye conditions.
- **X-rays and gamma rays**: mutation or damage to cells in the body.

## Communication
Communication with artificial satellites is mainly by microwaves.
- Some satellite phones use **low orbit** satellites.
- Some satellite phones and direct broadcast satellite television use **geostationary** satellites.

**Extended**
- **Mobile phones and wireless internet** use microwaves, because they penetrate some walls and only need a short aerial.
- **Bluetooth** uses radio waves, because they pass through walls, though the signal gets weaker.
- **Optical fibres** carry cable television and high-speed broadband. Glass is transparent to visible light and some infrared, and these can carry high rates of data.

## Digital and analogue signals (Extended)
- An **analogue** signal varies continuously.
- A **digital** signal has only two values, 1 and 0.
- Sound can be transmitted as either type.

**Benefits of digital signals**
- higher rate of data transmission
- longer range, because the signal can be regenerated accurately, removing noise

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Learn the order. As frequency goes up, wavelength goes down.
- Thermal radiation is infrared.
- Use v = fλ with v = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s for electromagnetic waves.
