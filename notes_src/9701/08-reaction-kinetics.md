# 8 Reaction kinetics (AS)

## Key definitions
- **Rate of reaction**: the change in concentration of a reactant or product per unit time.
- **Activation energy (Eₐ)**: the minimum energy colliding particles must have in order to react.
- **Catalyst**: a substance that **increases the rate** of a reaction without being chemically changed at the end, by providing an **alternative pathway with a lower activation energy**.
- **Homogeneous catalyst**: in the same phase as the reactants. **Heterogeneous catalyst**: in a different phase (e.g. a solid catalyst with gases).

## Collision theory
Particles react only if they **collide** with energy **≥ Eₐ** and with the correct **orientation**.
| Factor | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Concentration or pressure** increased | More particles per unit volume, so **more frequent collisions**, so more successful collisions per unit time |
| **Surface area** increased | More particles exposed, so more frequent collisions |
| **Temperature** increased | Particles have more kinetic energy, so a **much greater proportion have energy ≥ Eₐ** (the main reason); collisions are also more frequent |
| **Catalyst** added | A lower Eₐ, so a greater proportion of collisions have enough energy |

## Boltzmann distribution
- A graph of the number of molecules against energy. It starts at the origin, is not symmetrical, and never touches the energy axis. The area under the curve = the total number of molecules.
- **Higher temperature**: the peak moves **right** and **lower**, the curve flattens, and the area beyond Eₐ is **larger**. The total area is unchanged.
- **Catalyst**: the curve stays the same, but **Eₐ is marked further left**, so more molecules exceed it.

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Say "more **frequent** collisions" (per unit time), not just "more collisions".
- For temperature, the key point is the **greater proportion of molecules with energy ≥ Eₐ**.
- Draw Boltzmann curves carefully: start at 0,0, the high-temperature curve has a lower peak further right, and both tails approach the axis without touching it.
