# 10 Group 2 (Be → Ba)

## Trends down the group
- **Atomic radius increases** (more shells).
- **First and second ionisation energies decrease**: the outer electrons are further from the nucleus, with more shielding.
- **Reactivity increases**: it is easier to lose the two outer electrons.

## Reactions
- **With oxygen**: 2M + O₂ → 2MO (they burn more vigorously going down the group).
- **With water**: M + 2H₂O → M(OH)₂ + H₂, faster going down the group. Mg reacts very slowly with cold water; with steam, Mg + H₂O → MgO + H₂.
- **With dilute acids**: M + 2HCl → MCl₂ + H₂.
  - With H₂SO₄, the reaction **slows down or stops going down the group**, because the sulfate formed is **less soluble** (insoluble BaSO₄ coats the metal).
- **Oxides and hydroxides with water**: they form alkaline solutions; the pH increases going down the group because the hydroxides become more soluble.
- **Carbonates and nitrates** react with acids to give a salt + water (+ CO₂ for carbonates).

## Thermal decomposition
- MCO₃ → MO + CO₂
- 2M(NO₃)₂ → 2MO + 4NO₂ + O₂ (brown gas)
- **Thermal stability increases down the group**. The cation gets **larger**, so its **charge density** is smaller and it **polarises** (distorts) the carbonate or nitrate ion **less**, so the anion is harder to break up.

## Solubility trends
- **Hydroxides**: become **more** soluble down the group (Mg(OH)₂ is sparingly soluble; Ba(OH)₂ is fairly soluble).
- **Sulfates**: become **less** soluble down the group (MgSO₄ is soluble; BaSO₄ is insoluble).
- **Test for sulfate ions**: add acidified BaCl₂(aq), which gives a **white precipitate** of BaSO₄.

## Uses
- **Ca(OH)₂ (slaked lime)** and CaCO₃ (limestone): neutralise acidic soils and lakes.
- **Mg(OH)₂**: an antacid for indigestion.
- **CaCO₃**: making cement and glass; desulfurisation in power stations.

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Explain thermal stability using **cation size → charge density → polarisation** of the anion.
- Don't mix up the solubility trends: hydroxides become **more** soluble, sulfates **less** soluble.
- Learn the nitrate decomposition equation **with the brown NO₂**.
