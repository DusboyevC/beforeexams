# 1 Number

## Types of number
- **Natural numbers**: 1, 2, 3, …
- **Integers**: positive, zero and negative whole numbers.
- **Prime numbers**: have exactly two factors, 1 and themselves. 1 is not prime.
- **Square numbers**: 1, 4, 9, 16, …
- **Cube numbers**: 1, 8, 27, 64, …
- **Rational numbers**: can be written as a fraction. **Irrational numbers** cannot, such as √2 and π.
- **Reciprocal**: 1 ÷ the number. The reciprocal of 4 is ¼.

**Prime factors**: 72 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 2³ × 3².

**HCF and LCM** of 72 and 60, where 60 = 2² × 3 × 5:
- **HCF**: take the lowest powers of shared primes: 2² × 3 = **12**.
- **LCM**: take the highest power of every prime: 2³ × 3² × 5 = **360**.

## Sets
- **n(A)**: number of elements in A.
- **A′**: complement, what is not in A.
- **ℰ**: universal set.
- **A ∪ B**: union, in A or B or both.
- **A ∩ B**: intersection, in both A and B.

**Extended**: ∈ means 'is an element of', ∉ means 'is not', ∅ is the empty set, and A ⊆ B means A is a subset of B. Venn diagrams may use three sets.

## Powers, roots and indices
Learn squares up to 15² and cubes of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10.

**Rules**
- aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
- aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ
- (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
- a⁰ = 1
- a⁻ⁿ = 1 / aⁿ, so 7⁻² = 1/49

**Extended**
- a^(1/n) is the nth root of a, so 16^(1/4) = 2.
- a^(m/n) means take the root, then the power: 8^(2/3) = 2² = 4.
- 8^(−2/3) = 1/4.

## Fractions, decimals and percentages
- Convert to a common form to compare.
- **Extended, recurring decimals**: x = 0.1777…, so 10x = 1.777… and 100x = 17.777…. Subtract: 90x = 16, so x = 16/90 = **8/45**.

## Standard form
**A × 10ⁿ**, where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is an integer.
- 45 000 = 4.5 × 10⁴
- 0.0032 = 3.2 × 10⁻³

## Rounding and estimating
- Round 5764 to the nearest thousand: **6000**.
- Round to 1 significant figure to estimate: 41.3 ÷ (9.79 × 0.765) ≈ 40 ÷ (10 × 0.8) = **5**.

## Limits of accuracy
- A length of 12 m to the nearest metre has bounds **11.5 ≤ l < 12.5**.

**Extended, calculations with bounds**
- Maximum of a + b or a × b: use the upper bounds.
- Maximum of a − b or a ÷ b: use upper a with lower b.
- Example: distance 100 m (nearest m), time 12 s (nearest s). Lower bound of speed = 99.5 ÷ 12.5 = **7.96 m/s**.

## Ratio and proportion
- Simplify by dividing: 20 : 30 : 40 = 2 : 3 : 4.
- Share $60 in the ratio 2 : 3. One part = 60 ÷ 5 = 12. Shares: **$24 and $36**.

## Rates
- **Speed = distance ÷ time**.
- Example: 45 km in 3 hours 45 minutes. 45 ÷ 3.75 = **12 km/h**.
- Other rates: pay, exchange rates, flow rates, fuel consumption, pressure, density.

## Percentages
- Percentage of an amount: 15% of 80 = 0.15 × 80 = 12.
- As a percentage: 12 out of 80 = 12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15%.
- Percentage change = change ÷ original × 100.
- **Simple interest** = P × r × t ÷ 100. Example: $500 at 3% for 4 years gives $60.
- **Compound interest**: value = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ. Example: $500 at 3% for 4 years gives 500 × 1.03⁴ = **$562.75**.

**Extended, reverse percentages**: a price after 20% profit is $90. Original = 90 ÷ 1.2 = **$75**.

## Exponential growth and decay (Extended)
- Growth: value = start × (1 + r/100)ⁿ.
- Decay: value = start × (1 − r/100)ⁿ.
- Example: a $20 000 car loses 15% per year. After 3 years: 20 000 × 0.85³ = **$12 282.50**.

## Surds (Extended)
- Simplify: √20 = 2√5.
- √200 − √32 = 10√2 − 4√2 = **6√2**.
- Rationalise: 10 / √5 = 10√5 / 5 = **2√5**.
- Use the conjugate: 1 / (√3 − 1) = (√3 + 1) / ((√3 − 1)(√3 + 1)) = **(1 + √3) / 2**.

## Calculator, time and money
- Do not round during working. Round only the final answer.
- 3.25 hours is 3 hours 15 minutes, not 3 hours 25.
- 4.8 on a calculator means $4.80 in a money question.
- The 24-hour clock writes 3.15 p.m. as 15 15.

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Give answers to 3 significant figures, unless told otherwise, and angles to 1 decimal place.
- Percentage change always divides by the original value.
- Round money to 2 decimal places.
