# 10 Chemistry of the environment

## Water
**Testing for water**
- Anhydrous cobalt(II) chloride turns from **blue to pink**.
- Anhydrous copper(II) sulfate turns from **white to blue**.

**Testing purity**: pure water melts at exactly 0 °C and boils at exactly 100 °C.

**Distilled water** is used in practical chemistry, because it contains fewer chemical impurities than tap water.

**Substances in natural water**

| Beneficial | Potentially harmful |
|---|---|
| dissolved oxygen for aquatic life | some toxic metal compounds |
| some metal compounds provide essential minerals | plastics harm aquatic life |
| | sewage contains harmful microbes which cause disease |
| | nitrates and phosphates lead to deoxygenation and damage to aquatic life |

Nitrates come from fertilisers. Phosphates come from fertilisers and detergents.

**Treating the domestic water supply**
1. **Sedimentation and filtration** remove solids.
2. **Carbon** removes tastes and odours.
3. **Chlorination** kills microbes.

## Fertilisers
- Ammonium salts and nitrates are used as fertilisers.
- **NPK fertilisers** provide nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium for improved plant growth.

## Air
Clean, dry air is about **78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen**, and the remainder is noble gases and carbon dioxide.

| Pollutant | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon dioxide | complete combustion of carbon-containing fuels | increased global warming, leading to climate change |
| Carbon monoxide | incomplete combustion | toxic gas |
| Particulates | incomplete combustion | respiratory problems and cancer |
| Methane | decomposition of vegetation; waste gases from digestion in animals | increased global warming, leading to climate change |
| Oxides of nitrogen | car engines | acid rain, photochemical smog, respiratory problems |
| Sulfur dioxide | burning fossil fuels containing sulfur compounds | acid rain |

## Global warming (Extended)
1. The Earth's surface absorbs thermal energy from the Sun.
2. It emits energy as infrared.
3. Carbon dioxide and methane absorb this infrared and emit it again in all directions.
4. Less thermal energy is lost to space, so the atmosphere warms.

## Reducing the problems
**Climate change**
- planting trees
- reducing livestock farming
- using less fossil fuel
- using more hydrogen and renewable energy, such as wind and solar

**Acid rain**
- catalytic converters in vehicles
- low-sulfur fuels
- **flue gas desulfurisation** with calcium oxide

## Catalytic converters (Extended)
- Oxides of nitrogen form in car engines because the high temperature makes nitrogen and oxygen from the air react.
- A catalytic converter removes them: **2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂**

## Photosynthesis
- Carbon dioxide and water react to make glucose and oxygen, using light energy, in the presence of chlorophyll.
- Word equation: carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
- **Extended**: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Carbon monoxide comes from incomplete combustion, not complete combustion.
- Sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen cause acid rain. CO₂ and methane cause global warming.
- Name the correct source: NOₓ from car engines, SO₂ from sulfur in fuels.

## Quick self-check
1. What percentage of the air is oxygen? *(About 21%)*
2. What does chlorine do in water treatment? *(It kills microbes.)*
3. Name two gases that cause acid rain. *(Sulfur dioxide and oxides of nitrogen)*
