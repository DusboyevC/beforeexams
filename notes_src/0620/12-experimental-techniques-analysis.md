# 12 Experimental techniques and chemical analysis

## Apparatus
- **Time**: stop-watch.
- **Temperature**: thermometer.
- **Mass**: balance.
- **Volume**: burette, volumetric pipette, measuring cylinder, gas syringe.

A burette and a pipette are more accurate than a measuring cylinder.

## Key words
- **Solvent**: dissolves a solute.
- **Solute**: is dissolved in a solvent.
- **Solution**: a mixture of one or more solutes dissolved in a solvent.
- **Saturated solution**: contains the maximum concentration of solute at a specified temperature.
- **Residue**: remains after evaporation, distillation, filtration or a similar process.
- **Filtrate**: the liquid or solution that has passed through a filter.

## Acid–base titration
1. Use a **volumetric pipette** to put a fixed volume of alkali in a conical flask.
2. Add a few drops of a suitable **indicator**.
3. Add acid from a **burette**, swirling the flask, until the indicator just changes colour. This is the **end-point**.
4. Record the volume, and repeat until the results are close.

## Chromatography
- Draw a pencil line on the paper and add spots of the samples. Stand the paper in a suitable solvent, with the solvent below the line.
- Soluble substances move up the paper at different speeds.
- **Identifying**: spots at the same height as a known substance show the same substance.
- **Purity**: a pure substance gives one spot.
- **Colourless substances (Extended)**: use a locating agent to show the spots.
- **Extended**: **Rf = distance travelled by substance ÷ distance travelled by solvent**.

Use pencil for the line, because ink would dissolve and move up the paper.

## Separation and purification
| Method | Use |
|---|---|
| Suitable solvent | dissolves one substance and leaves another |
| Filtration | separates an insoluble solid from a liquid |
| Crystallisation | obtains a solid from its solution |
| Simple distillation | obtains a solvent from a solution |
| Fractional distillation | separates liquids with different boiling points |

**Purity**: a pure substance has a sharp melting and boiling point. Impurities lower the melting point and raise the boiling point.

## Tests for anions
| Anion | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Carbonate | add dilute acid, test the gas | CO₂ turns limewater milky |
| Chloride | dilute nitric acid, then aqueous silver nitrate | white precipitate |
| Bromide | same | cream precipitate |
| Iodide | same | yellow precipitate |
| Nitrate | aluminium foil and aqueous NaOH, warm | ammonia gas |
| Sulfate | dilute nitric acid, then aqueous barium nitrate | white precipitate |
| Sulfite | acidified aqueous potassium manganate(VII) | purple to colourless |

## Tests for cations
| Cation | Aqueous NaOH | Aqueous ammonia |
|---|---|---|
| Al³⁺ | white ppt, soluble in excess | white ppt, insoluble in excess |
| NH₄⁺ | ammonia on warming | not applicable |
| Ca²⁺ | white ppt, insoluble in excess | no ppt, or very slight white ppt |
| Cr³⁺ | green ppt, soluble in excess | green ppt, insoluble in excess |
| Cu²⁺ | light blue ppt, insoluble in excess | light blue ppt, soluble in excess giving a dark blue solution |
| Fe²⁺ | green ppt, insoluble in excess | green ppt, insoluble in excess |
| Fe³⁺ | red-brown ppt, insoluble in excess | red-brown ppt, insoluble in excess |
| Zn²⁺ | white ppt, soluble in excess | white ppt, soluble in excess |

## Tests for gases
| Gas | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Ammonia | damp red litmus paper | turns blue |
| Carbon dioxide | limewater | turns milky |
| Chlorine | damp litmus paper | bleaches it |
| Hydrogen | lighted splint | squeaky pop |
| Oxygen | glowing splint | relights |
| Sulfur dioxide | acidified aqueous potassium manganate(VII) | purple to colourless |

| Li⁺ | red |
| Na⁺ | yellow |
| K⁺ | lilac |
| Ca²⁺ | orange-red |
| Ba²⁺ | light green |

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Say 'white precipitate', not only 'white'.
- Say whether the precipitate dissolves in excess.
- Use nitric acid in halide tests. Hydrochloric acid would add chloride ions.

## Quick self-check
1. A spot moves 3.0 cm and the solvent 6.0 cm. Find Rf. *(0.50)*
2. A solution gives a light blue precipitate with NaOH. Name the cation. *(Cu²⁺)*
3. Which gas turns limewater milky? *(Carbon dioxide)*
