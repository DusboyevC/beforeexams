# 13 Excretion in humans

## What is excreted
- **Carbon dioxide** is excreted through the lungs.
- The **kidneys** excrete **urea**, and excess water and ions.

## The urinary system
- **Kidneys**: filter the blood and make urine.
- **Ureters**: carry urine to the bladder.
- **Bladder**: stores urine.
- **Urethra**: carries urine out of the body.

**Extended**: a kidney has an outer **cortex** and an inner **medulla**.

## The nephron (Extended)
1. **Filtration**: in the **glomerulus**, water, glucose, urea and ions are filtered from the blood.
2. **Reabsorption**: **all** of the glucose, **some** of the ions and **most** of the water go back into the blood.
3. **Urine forms**: it contains urea, excess water and excess ions.

## The liver (Extended)
- **Assimilation**: the liver converts amino acids to proteins.
- **Deamination**: the removal of the nitrogen-containing part of amino acids, forming **urea**. Urea is formed in the liver from excess amino acids.
- **Why excretion matters**: urea is **toxic**, so it must be removed.

## Exam tips and common mistakes
- Urea is made in the liver and removed by the kidneys.
- Protein molecules and blood cells are too large to be filtered, so they stay in the blood.
- Glucose is fully reabsorbed, so it is normally absent from urine.

## Quick self-check
1. Which organ makes urea? *(Liver)*
2. Name the tube from the kidney to the bladder. *(Ureter)*
3. Which substance is all reabsorbed? *(Glucose)*
