IGCSE Physics 0625

6 Energy, work and power

Energy stores

Energy may be stored as kinetic, gravitational potential, chemical, elastic (strain), nuclear, electrostatic and internal (thermal).

Energy is transferred between stores by:

  • forces (mechanical work done)
  • electrical currents (electrical work done)
  • heating
  • electromagnetic, sound and other waves

Conservation of energy

  • Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be transferred between stores.
  • Extended: Sankey diagrams show transfers. The width of each arrow shows the amount of energy.

Energy equations (Extended)

  • Kinetic energy: Eₖ = ½mv²
  • Change in gravitational potential energy: ΔEₚ = mgΔh

Worked example: a 2.0 kg ball drops 5.0 m. Take g = 9.8 N/kg and ignore air resistance.

  • ΔEₚ = 2.0 × 9.8 × 5.0 = 98 J, and this all becomes kinetic energy.
  • 98 = ½ × 2.0 × v², so v² = 98 and v = 9.9 m/s.

Work

  • Mechanical or electrical work done equals the energy transferred.
  • W = Fd = ΔE. Unit: J. Here d is the distance moved in the direction of the force.

Power

  • Power is work done per unit time, or energy transferred per unit time.
  • P = W / t and P = ΔE / t. Unit: W, where 1 W = 1 J/s.

Efficiency

  • Efficiency tells you how much of the input energy becomes useful.
  • Extended: (%) efficiency = (useful energy output ÷ total energy input) × 100%.
  • Extended: (%) efficiency = (useful power output ÷ total power input) × 100%.
  • Efficiency can never be above 100%.

Energy resources

Fuel-burning and nuclear power stations: chemical energy in fossil fuels or biofuels, or nuclear fuel, heats water in a boiler. Steam spins a turbine, which turns a generator.

Resource Advantages Disadvantages
Fossil fuels reliable, large scale, available on demand non-renewable, release CO₂ and other pollution
Biofuels renewable, can be grown use farmland, release CO₂ when burned
Nuclear large scale, reliable, no CO₂ while running non-renewable, radioactive waste, risk of accidents
Hydroelectric renewable, reliable, quick to start floods land, needs suitable valleys
Tidal renewable, predictable few sites, affects habitats
Waves renewable unreliable, small scale
Wind renewable, no fuel cost depends on wind, noise, changes the view
Geothermal renewable, reliable only in some places
Solar cells renewable, no moving parts depends on sunlight, high set-up cost
Solar panels renewable, heat water cheaply depend on sunlight

Extended facts

  • Radiation from the Sun is the main source of energy for all our energy resources, except geothermal, nuclear and tidal.
  • Energy is released by nuclear fusion in the Sun.
  • Research is being carried out into using fusion to produce electrical energy on a large scale.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Square the velocity, not the whole of ½mv.
  • Solar cells make electricity from light. Solar panels heat water using infrared and other waves.
  • Name the actual stores in your answer, for example chemical to kinetic.

Quick self-check

  1. A 500 N force pushes a crate 4.0 m. Find the work done. (2000 J)
  2. That work takes 8.0 s. Find the power. (250 W)
  3. A motor takes in 400 J and gives out 300 J of useful energy. Find its efficiency. (75%)
