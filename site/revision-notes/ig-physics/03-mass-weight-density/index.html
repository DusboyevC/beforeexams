3 Mass, weight and density

Mass and weight

  • Mass is a measure of the quantity of matter in an object at rest relative to the observer. Unit: kg.
  • Weight is a gravitational force on an object that has mass. Unit: N.
  • Gravitational field strength is force per unit mass: g = W / m. Near the Earth, g ≈ 9.8 N/kg.
  • This value of g is equivalent to the acceleration of free fall.
  • Weights, and so masses, can be compared using a balance.
  • Extended: weight is the effect of a gravitational field on a mass.

Mass stays the same everywhere. Weight changes when g changes. On the Moon g is about 1.6 N/kg, so a 60 kg astronaut weighs about 96 N there but about 590 N on Earth.

Density

  • Density is mass per unit volume: ρ = m / V. Units: kg/m³ or g/cm³.
  • 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³.

Measuring density

  • Liquid: find the mass of an empty measuring cylinder, add liquid, and find the mass again. Subtract to get the liquid's mass. Read the volume from the cylinder.
  • Regular solid: find the mass on a balance. Measure the sides and calculate the volume, for example l × w × h for a cuboid.
  • Irregular solid that sinks: find the mass on a balance. Find the volume by displacement. Put water in a measuring cylinder, lower the object in, and subtract the two readings.

Floating

  • An object floats if its density is less than the density of the liquid.
  • It sinks if its density is greater.
  • Extended: if two liquids do not mix, the less dense liquid floats on the denser one.

Worked example

A stone has a mass of 54 g. The water level rises from 40 cm³ to 60 cm³.

  • Volume = 60 − 40 = 20 cm³
  • Density = 54 / 20 = 2.7 g/cm³
  • Water's density is 1.0 g/cm³, so the stone sinks.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Never say an object weighs 5 kg in a physics answer. Mass is in kg and weight is in N.
  • Keep units matched: g with cm³, or kg with m³.
  • For displacement, the object must be fully under the water.

Quick self-check

  1. Find the weight of a 2.5 kg bag on Earth. (24.5 N)
  2. A block of 2.0 cm × 3.0 cm × 5.0 cm has a mass of 240 g. Find its density. (8.0 g/cm³)
  3. Does an object with density 0.9 g/cm³ float on water? (Yes)
