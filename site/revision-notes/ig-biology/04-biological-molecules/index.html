IGCSE Biology 0610

4 Biological molecules

Elements

Molecule Elements
Carbohydrates carbon, hydrogen, oxygen
Fats carbon, hydrogen, oxygen
Proteins carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen (and sometimes sulfur)

Large molecules from small ones

  • Starch, glycogen and cellulose are made from glucose.
  • Proteins are made from amino acids.
  • Fats and oils are made from fatty acids and glycerol.

Food tests

Test for Method Positive result
Starch add iodine solution orange-brown to blue-black
Reducing sugars add Benedict's solution and heat blue to green, yellow or brick-red
Proteins add biuret solution blue to purple
Fats and oils shake with ethanol, then add water clear to a cloudy white emulsion
Vitamin C add the food to DCPIP solution blue to colourless

For Benedict's, more reducing sugar gives a colour further along towards brick-red.

DNA

  • Two strands are coiled together to form a double helix.
  • Each strand contains chemicals called bases.
  • Bonds between pairs of bases hold the strands together.
  • Bases always pair the same way: A with T and C with G.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Benedict's solution must be heated in a water bath.
  • Give the starting colour as well as the final colour.
  • Do not say a food 'contains glucose' from a Benedict's test alone. It shows reducing sugars.

Quick self-check

  1. Which test uses ethanol? (The emulsion test for fats and oils)
  2. Which base pairs with C? (G)
  3. Which element is in proteins but not in carbohydrates? (Nitrogen)
