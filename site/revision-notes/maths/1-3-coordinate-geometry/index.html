AS & A Level Mathematics 9709

1.3 Coordinate geometry

Straight lines

  • Gradient: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).
  • Equation: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
  • Midpoint: ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).
  • Distance: √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²).
  • Parallel lines: equal gradients. Perpendicular lines: m₁m₂ = −1.
  • Perpendicular bisector of AB: passes through the midpoint of AB, with gradient −1/m_AB.

Circles

  • A circle with centre (a, b) and radius r: (x − a)² + (y − b)² = r².
  • From the expanded form x² + y² + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0, complete the square in x and y to find the centre (−g, −f) and radius √(g² + f² − c).
  • A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
  • The perpendicular bisector of a chord passes through the centre.
  • An angle in a semicircle is 90°: if AB is a diameter and P is on the circle, then AP ⊥ BP.

Typical tasks

  • Equation of a tangent at a point P: find the gradient of the radius CP, take the negative reciprocal, and use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
  • Line meets circle: substitute the line into the circle to get a quadratic; the discriminant tells you whether it intersects, touches (tangent) or misses.
  • Circle through three points: the centre is where the perpendicular bisectors of two chords meet.

Worked example

Find the equation of the tangent to x² + y² − 4x + 6y − 12 = 0 at P(5, 1).

  • Complete the square: (x − 2)² + (y + 3)² = 25, so the centre is C(2, −3) and r = 5.
  • Gradient of CP = (1 + 3)/(5 − 2) = 4/3, so the gradient of the tangent = −3/4.
  • y − 1 = −¾(x − 5), which gives 3x + 4y − 19 = 0.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Find the centre and radius by completing the square, and check the signs.
  • Use exact values (surds and fractions) unless told otherwise.
  • Show gradient products = −1 when proving lines are perpendicular.

Quick self-check

  1. Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of A(1, 2) and B(5, 6). (y = −x + 7)
  2. Find the centre and radius of x² + y² + 6x − 2y − 15 = 0. ((−3, 1), 5)
  3. Show that y = x + 1 is a tangent to (x − 2)² + y² = 4.5.
