Questions

What is BeforeExams?

BeforeExams is a free revision platform for Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level students. It has past papers with mark schemes, topical questions sorted by syllabus topic, revision notes, timed mock tests and score tracking.

Who made BeforeExams?

BeforeExams was made by DusboyevC and Magnetars.

Which subjects are included?

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, at both IGCSE and AS & A Level.

Which years are included?

Cambridge series from 2015 to 2026: Feb/March, May/June and Oct/Nov, wherever Cambridge published the paper.

Is BeforeExams free?

Yes. Revision notes are open to everyone. Past papers and topical PDFs ask for the site password, and logged-in students are asked once every 15 days.

Is BeforeExams affiliated with Cambridge?

No. BeforeExams is an independent study site. Past papers and mark schemes are © Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
