7 Transformations and vectors

The four transformations

Use one word and give all the details.

Transformation Details to give
Reflection the mirror line, such as x = 2 or y = x
Rotation the centre, the angle and the direction
Enlargement the centre and the scale factor
Translation the column vector

Example answer: 'Rotation, 90° clockwise, centre (0, 0).'

Enlargement

  • Scale factor 2: each point is twice as far from the centre.
  • Scale factor ½: the shape gets smaller, and points are half as far.
  • Extended: a negative scale factor puts the image on the opposite side of the centre.

Translation: written as a column vector with x at the top and y at the bottom. (x, y) here means x right and y up.

Combinations (Extended): carry out the first transformation, then the second on the image. Then describe the single transformation if asked.

Vectors (Extended)

A vector has size and direction. It can be written as a column vector, or as a, or as AB with an arrow.

In this note, a column vector is written as (x, y).

Add and subtract

  • (3, 4) + (1, −2) = (4, 2)
  • (3, 4) − (1, −2) = (2, 6)

Multiply by a scalar

  • 2 × (3, 4) = (6, 8)
  • A vector −a has the same size as a, in the opposite direction.

Magnitude

  • |(x, y)| = √(x² + y²)
  • |(3, 4)| = √(9 + 16) = 5

Position vectors: OA is the position vector of point A, starting at the origin.

Vector geometry (Extended)

Route rule: go along known vectors from start to finish.

  • AB = AO + OB = −a + b = b − a

Example: OA = a and OB = b. M is the midpoint of AB.

  • AB = b − a
  • AM = ½(b − a)
  • OM = OA + AM = a + ½(b − a) = ½a + ½b

Parallel: one vector is a multiple of another. If AB = 2a + 4b and CD = a + 2b, then AB = 2 × CD, so they are parallel.

Collinear points: if AB is a multiple of BC, and they share the point B, then A, B and C lie on a straight line.

Ratio: if P divides AB in the ratio 1 : 3, then AP = ¼AB.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Do not give two transformations for one movement, unless the question asks.
  • Never describe it with loose words like 'turn' or 'flip'.
  • In vector paths, going backwards along a vector changes its sign.
