16 Hydroxy compounds (alcohols)

Making alcohols

  • Hydration of an alkene with steam (H₃PO₄ catalyst).
  • Hydrolysis of a halogenoalkane with NaOH(aq).
  • Reduction of aldehydes or ketones with NaBH₄.
  • Fermentation of glucose with yeast (anaerobic, about 35 °C): C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂.

Properties

  • Alcohols have high boiling points and short-chain alcohols are soluble in water, because they form hydrogen bonds (between –OH groups, and with water).

Reactions

Reaction Reagent and conditions Product
Combustion O₂ CO₂ + H₂O
With sodium Na at room temperature Sodium alkoxide + H₂ (fizzing)
Substitution to a halogenoalkane HX; PCl₃ or PCl₅; SOCl₂ R–X
Dehydration Heat with concentrated H₂SO₄ or H₃PO₄, or pass the vapour over hot Al₂O₃ Alkene + H₂O
Esterification Carboxylic acid + concentrated H₂SO₄ catalyst, heat Ester + water
Oxidation Acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ (orange → green), heat See below

Oxidation

  • Primary alcohol → aldehyde: distil it off as it forms. With excess oxidant and heating under reflux, it is oxidised further to a carboxylic acid.
  • Secondary alcohol → ketone (heat under reflux).
  • Tertiary alcohol: not oxidised (there is no H on the C carrying the OH), so the dichromate stays orange.

Tri-iodomethane test: alkaline I₂ (warm) gives a yellow precipitate of CHI₃ with alcohols containing the CH₃CH(OH)– group (e.g. ethanol, propan-2-ol). It also works for methyl ketones.

Acidity

  • Alcohols are weaker acids than water: the alkyl group donates electrons and destabilises the alkoxide ion.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Distinguish the conditions: distil to collect the aldehyde; reflux for the carboxylic acid.
  • Give the colour change of dichromate as orange → green, and state that a tertiary alcohol gives no change.
  • Know the tri-iodomethane test structure: CH₃CH(OH)– or CH₃CO–.

Quick self-check

  1. Name the product when butan-2-ol is heated under reflux with acidified K₂Cr₂O₇. (butanone)
  2. Give a chemical test to distinguish propan-1-ol from propan-2-ol. (tri-iodomethane test: yellow precipitate with propan-2-ol only)
  3. Write the equation for the dehydration of ethanol. (C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O)
