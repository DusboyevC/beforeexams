15 Drugs

What a drug is

A drug is any substance taken into the body that modifies or affects chemical reactions in the body.

Antibiotics

  • Antibiotics treat bacterial infections.
  • They kill bacteria but do not affect viruses.
  • Some bacteria are resistant to antibiotics, which reduces the effectiveness of antibiotics.

Limiting resistance (Extended)

  • Resistant bacteria such as MRSA develop by natural selection. Bacteria with a resistance gene survive and reproduce.
  • Using antibiotics only when essential gives resistant bacteria fewer chances to be selected, so they spread less.
  • Completing the full course also helps, as more of the bacteria are killed.

Exam tips and common mistakes

  • Bacteria become resistant, not people.
  • Do not use antibiotics for colds or flu, as these are caused by viruses.

Quick self-check

  1. Why do antibiotics not cure flu? (Flu is caused by a virus.)
  2. What is MRSA? (A bacterium resistant to antibiotics.)
