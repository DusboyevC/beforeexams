Privacy policy

BeforeExams is a free study site. This page says what we keep, why, and how to remove it. Last updated 1 November 2026.

What you can do without an account

You can browse the library and read revision notes without giving us anything. We count visits as daily totals only: no names, emails or IP addresses go into those totals.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Your email address and a protected form of your password, if you set one.
  • The level, subjects, exam dates and target you choose to add.
  • The scores and mock results you log.

We use these only to run your account and show your own progress. Other students can’t see them. We don’t sell your data or show adverts.

Mistake reports

If you report a mistake, we keep what you wrote and which paper it was about. The report isn’t linked to your account, so please don’t put personal details in it.

Saved on your device

The site saves a few settings in your browser, such as your theme, your last subject, your login and a running mock test. You can clear them in your browser settings. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and scores.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare hosts the past paper files.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

These services handle data only so the site can work, and they may process it outside your country.

Deleting your data

Email us from your account’s address and we’ll delete your account and scores. If you are under the age where your country requires a parent’s permission, please ask a parent or guardian before making an account.
