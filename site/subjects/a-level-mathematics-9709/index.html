Exam structure

  • AS Level: Papers 1 and 4, or Papers 1 and 5, or Papers 1 and 2. The Papers 1 and 2 route is AS only and can't carry forward to A Level.
  • A Level in one series: Papers 1, 3, 4 and 5, or Papers 1, 3, 5 and 6.
  • A Level over two years: Papers 1 and 4, then 3 and 5. Or Papers 1 and 5, then 3 and 4. Or Papers 1 and 5, then 3 and 6.
